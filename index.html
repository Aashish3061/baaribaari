How it works

AI proposes. Your family decides.

Describe your family

Where each sibling lives, when they're free, the time and money they can offer, how they like to help, and what your parent needs. No names.

AI suggests a split, with reasons

Each sibling gets tasks and a one-line "why" based only on what you entered. Code checks every task is covered and in-person tasks stay with local siblings.

Edit, swap, re-explain

Move any task to another sibling. The AI keeps your choices and rewrites the reasons and the family message.

Save and share

Save the plan, share its link in the family group, and mark it agreed once everyone says yes.

Principles

A starting proposal, never a verdict.

No scorecards

We don't rate siblings or call anyone unfair. Remote work is credited alongside who lives nearby.

No medical advice

BaariBaari plans who does what. Medicines, symptoms and doses belong with your parent's doctor. In an emergency in India, call 112.

No names stored

Siblings are A, B, C, D. Your free-text note is used once and never saved. Arithmetic like cost shares is done in code, not by the AI.

Questions

Good to know

Does the AI decide who does what?

No. It suggests a starting split with a short reason for each sibling. Your family moves tasks around, the AI re-explains, and you save the version everyone agrees on.

How is the care load estimated?

In code, not by the AI. Each task has a default weight: times a month × hours each × effort (2 for tasks needing physical presence or travel, 1 otherwise). A weekly visit (4 × 2 h × 2 = 16 points) counts far more than a weekly call (4 × 0.5 h × 1 = 2). Shares above 125% of an equal share show as High and below 75% as Low. These weights are directional defaults, not measured data.

What does "remotely doable" mean?

Six of the nine tasks (calls, bookings, bills and paperwork, online groceries, arranging repairs, the monthly review call) are classified as doable from another city in BaariBaari's task list. The headline percentage is the share of all tasks families ticked that fall in those six. It is directional and not representative of all families.

What do you store?

Your fixed-field choices, the AI's reply and token counts, and any plan you choose to save (siblings as A-D, tasks, reasons, message). We never store names or your free-text note.

How is the cost split worked out?

In code, from the money band each sibling chose (none, some, a larger share), rounded so it always adds up to 100%. It is separate from the care-load estimate.

Can it help with medicines or symptoms?

No, by design. For health questions, please speak to your parent's doctor. For non-emergency support for older persons, India's national Elderline is 14567; for emergencies, call 112.
